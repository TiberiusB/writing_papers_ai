# STRUCTURES of a publication

Construct the 3 following structural layers: **Thematic**, **Pragmatic** and **Logical/Emotional**. 

This will make the skeleton of the publication.

A **Thematic** structure is a bullet point form with items that respond to the question what are we talking about here?

A *Pragmatic** structure is a bullet point form with items that respond to the question what do we want to achieve here?

A **Logical/Emotional** structure is a bullet point form with items that respond to the question *what do we demonstrate or prove here*, or *what emotions are we passing here*. So here you lay down your argumentation or emotional content. 

These 3 structures can be merged together into one pointform structure, with the 3 layers separated by "/".

EXAMPLE of the 3 structures merged together:

* Introduction / open the subject and provide some context / funnel logic
    * The collaborative economy / introduce a general topic / from the most general observations, trends
    * P2p technologies / expose deep underlying causes of shift to collaborative economy / to some hypothesized causes 
    * Infrastructures / show manifestations of new tech / to some manifestations and empirical observations
    * SENSORICA/ make is concrete, provide an example / to some concrete incarnation of new forms of organization, the synthesis and consequences of these mew trends. 
* Development 
    * History of p2p tech / build a story, provide historical context, show that things come from somewhere / descriptive
        * Bit torrent 
        * Bitcoin
        * Ethereum
    * p2p socioeconomic patterns / provide some synthesis and analysis / synthesis of empirical observations
    * ...
* Conclusion / call to action / emotional logic
    * Past / show how far we’ve come, generate sense of pride below / sad
    * Present / make people comfortable with what there is today / action
    * Future / a call to action, build confidence and optimism / happy



# IMPACT

Content is written with a specific audiance in mind and serves a purposes. It also acts at the interface between different groups of stakeholders. 

Content can have an impact on those who produce it (their reputation for example), as it is used by other actors to evaluate the potential of those who produce the content, the probability to establish relations , etc. 

Content producers must be mindful of different types of impacts and insert within the content features that help maximize this impact. 

In order to maximize the impact of the content (deliverable), the content must exhibit *synergy hooks* (must be explicit about how this is useful to the audiance, value propositions, etc.) and **viral aspects**, to spread through social media channels. Incentive mechanisms can be attached to content so that is reaches key actors within the audiance eosystem, the ones that can use it and in doing so actualize potential for the network. 

(A *synergy hook* is a point of interface between two agents (individuals, orgs) that generates benefits for both and provides incentives to act together, for example to endorse or propagate a content.)
