# Methodology and Human Contribution

This document describes how "The History of Sensorica: From Seed Value to Open Value Network" was produced and what role the human author played in the process.

## 1. Writing methodology

Pipeline: Researcher, Outliner, Drafter, Reviewer, Fact-checker, Editor, Human-input.

For each step:
- Researcher: Gathered sources from Sensorica website, Zotero Group Library (Blogs), and Zotero My Library (In the media). Produced research_log.md with 63 retained sources, Key Quotes, Key Facts/Data, and initial CLAIMS CHECKLIST.
- Outliner: Built structure from research_log.md. Produced outline.md with section purposes, key points, evidence mapping, Impact and Hooks, Notes for Drafter, and Open Questions and Gaps.
- Drafter: Turned outline into first full draft using only evidence from research_log.md. Produced draft.md.
- Reviewer: Evaluated draft for structure, clarity, gaps, and flow. Produced review_notes.md; added one transition in draft.md.
- Fact-checker: Verified every substantive claim against research_log.md. Produced claims_checklist.md (52 claims, 51 verified) and fact_check_report.md.
- Editor: Applied final polish (language clarity, attribution phrasing). Produced publish.md and edit_notes.md.
- Human-input: Produced this methodology_and_human_contribution.md.

Final deliverable: publish.md (the article) and methodology_and_human_contribution.md (this document).

## 3. Delineation: human vs AI contribution

AI contribution (by step):
- Researcher: Source gathering, extraction of quotes and facts, structuring into research_log.md.
- Outliner: Structure design, section purposes, evidence mapping from research.
- Drafter: Prose composition from outline and research.
- Reviewer: Structure/clarity evaluation, flow edit.
- Fact-checker: Claim verification against sources, checklist completion.
- Editor: Language polish, attribution phrasing.

Human contribution:
- Goals: Topic, audience, purpose, tone, constraints, materials.
- Scope: Extended research to all documents, In the media, 50+ sources.
- Content: Corrected historical facts (Hyperion/Tactus, exchange firm, Custodian roles).
- Framing: Mission and revenue metrics (material peer production, metric mismatch); Brastaviceanu quote interpretations.
- Terminology: "benefits" vs "value"; ventures list; mission wording.
- Evidence: Supplied IASC 2023 source to close fact-check gap.

Hybrid (co-constructed):
- Gap completion (Outliner): Human provided clarifications; AI propagated to outline, research_log, draft.
- Brastaviceanu quotes: Human supplied meaning; AI wove into draft.
- Direct edits: Human edited draft; AI logged in human_input_log and propagated to supporting docs.

## 4. Cognitive processes identified in the human

- Goal-setting: Defined topic, audience, tone, constraints, materials (Researcher).
- Ethical constraint: Set evidence requirements, word limits, tone (Researcher).
- Selection: Chose which materials to use, extended scope, shortened ventures list, removed WorkParty (Researcher, Direct edits).
- Reframing: Changed research scope, clarified In the media location (Researcher). Redefined mission and success metrics (Outliner).
- Correction: Fixed Hyperion/Tactus narrative, terminology (benefits), mission wording (Drafter, Direct edits). Supplied IASC 2023 source (Fact-checker).
- Synthesis: Interpreted Brastaviceanu quotes; integrated Tactus and revenue framing (Outliner, Draft clarification).
- Value framing: Mission of Sensorica, success metrics, metric mismatch (Outliner).
- Meaning-making: Quote interpretations, narrative coherence (Draft clarification).
- Metacognitive oversight: Constraint updates, gap completion, evidence supplementation (Researcher, Outliner, Fact-checker).
- Creative direction: Tone, ventures list, wording choices (Direct edits).

Pattern: Human set goals early (Researcher) and refined scope (constraint updates, In the media). Human filled gaps and corrected facts at Outliner and Drafter stages. Human made direct edits and supplied evidence at Fact-checker stage. No human input during Reviewer or Editor.

## 5. Recommendations for transparency and co-evolution

- The human_input_log captured input well. Consider logging any style or tone preferences given to the Editor for future runs.
- The pattern of gap completion (Outliner) and post-draft correction (Drafter) shows value in human review before and after key steps. Continuing to provide domain expertise at these stages will improve accuracy and framing.
- Supplying sources to close fact-check gaps (IASC 2023) is an effective way to maintain traceability without changing the draft's claims.
