# Title

The History of Sensorica: From Seed Value to Open Value Network

## Audience and Purpose

- Audience: People interested in economics, society, and peer-to-peer models, with at least a college degree. Readers likely care about alternative economic models, commons-based production, and open innovation.
- Purpose: Provide a short historical overview of Sensorica that informs readers about its evolution, key milestones, and inflection points; showcases the sacrifice, hard work, and creativity of its members; and inspires trust in the OVN model and interest in joining or adopting it.

## High-Level Summary

Sensorica went online on February 11, 2011, two years after Bitcoin, as an effort to understand how open networks can engage in material peer production. From a seed value (an optical fiber-based tactile sensor prototype) and early tensions between open and closed approaches, the network emerged as an Open Value Network (OVN) with a three-pillar structure: an NPO for governance, a network of companies for commercialization, and an open international community for innovation. Key inflection points include the 2011 restructuring (Tactus compromise: company to interface with market; Hyperion proposed, Tactus chosen), the 2015 White House presentation, the 2016 Ottawa meeting with the Canadian government, and sustained academic recognition. Today, Sensorica operates as a stigmergic environment for synergistic open innovation, with ventures like PEP Master and Decanter Centrifuge, a Montreal lab administered as a commons, and influence on international standards (ISO 15944-15).

### 3. The OVN Model Takes Shape (2012–2014)

Section purpose: Explain the three-pillar structure, contribution accounting, governance (nondominium, custodian, exchange firm), and how Sensorica identifies itself.

Key points:
- Three pillars (Tremblay & Yagoubi): (1) association/NPO for governance, (2) network of companies for commercialization, (3) open international community for collaborative work and innovation (Source 51)
- Sensorica as Fab Lab, Maker Space, incubator, accelerator combined in open value network (Source 51)
- Contribution accounting: record, evaluate, redistribute fairly; long-tail distribution; NRP-CAS (Sources 6, 51)
- Governance: nondominium, custodian (CAKE), open community, exchange firm (Sources 7, 8, 53)
- CAKE (Canadian Academy for the Knowledge Economy) pays rent and insurance for lab; no one owns Sensorica (Source 53)
- Exchange firm: legal entity that interfaces OVN with the market; absorbs liability for products; ensures fair access to market (OVN wiki). Sensorica first implemented this concept with Tactus (no longer in operation) to acquire funding and commercialize the Mosquito; later BDanConcepts for 3D printing services (OVN wiki Exchange firm)
- Tactus: company (Hyperion proposed, Tactus chosen) became Sensorica's exchange firm; for a while also played Custodian role; to interface primarily with funding institutions for R&D, and with market at later stage; Quebec/Fujitsu prize 2012 and Ingenio Tactus prize linked to this effort (Source 55, 60; human input; https://ovn.world/index.php?title=Exchange_firm)
- Quebec entrepreneurship/Fujitsu prize 2012; Ingenio Tactus prize; HEC Montréal 2012 (Source 55, 60)
