# Research Log: History of Sensorica

## RESEARCH TOPIC

A short historical overview of the Sensorica organization: its evolution from founding to present, key milestones and achievements, historical inflection points, and the philosophy behind its OVN (Open Value Network) economic model. The goal is to inform an audience interested in economics, society, and p2p about Sensorica, showcase the sacrifice, hard work, and creativity of its members, and inspire trust and adoption of the model.

### Source 53: Ettlinger: Cautious hope: Prospects and perils of communitarian governance in a Web3 environment

- **Author/Organization**: Nancy Ettlinger (Ohio State University)
- **URL**: https://linkinghub.elsevier.com/retrieve/pii/S2666378324000023
- **Date**: June 2024 | **Journal**: Digital Geography and Society, 6, 100080
- **Type**: Journal article | **Authority Level**: Secondary (academic)
- **Relevance**: Sensorica vs FairCoop; Montreal 2011 founding; CAKE custodian; exchange firms; challenges (revenue, freeloading); 5% fee response.

**Summary**: Compares FairCoop and Sensorica as CBPP case studies. Sensorica established Montreal 2011; on-demand agile network; market-oriented; federation of fluidly constituted networks. Legal custodian: Canadian Academy for the Knowledge Economy (CAKE), non-profit that pays rent and insurance for lab. No one owns Sensorica; lab shared. Exchange firms: hardware prototyping/fabrication, blockchain-related services. Challenges: revenue insufficient for living wage; freeloading (members/non-members using resources without contributing). Sensorica responded with 5% fee on revenue from mainstream projects using Sensorica space and resources.

**Key Quotes**:
- "Sensorica, still in operation, differs dramatically from FairCoop. It was established in Montreal in 2011 as an on-demand, agile network of [makers] for open-source hardware and automated sensors and sensing systems."
- "Sensorica's legal custodian is the non-profit Canadian Academy for the Knowledge Economy, which pays rent and insurance for Sensorica's material domain, the lab for developing the open-source hardware. No one therefore owns Sensorica."
- "Sensorica connects with the market and government through 2 types of 'exchange firms' it created: one type specializes in hardware prototyping and fabrication, and the other in blockchain-related services."
- "In the case of Sensorica, the revenue that is redistributed among the membership is insufficient to support a living wage."
- "Sensorica responded to the problems by eventually imposing a fee of 5% of revenue from mainstream projects using Sensorica space and resources."
